Part to Whole in HTML and JavaScript

These charts show how a whole splits into parts: how people paid, where money goes, what fills a drive. Use a pie or donut for a few simple shares, a waffle when you want people to picture the numbers, and a treemap, sunburst or icicle for nested data. Each chart is one HTML file with no library.
